The dose-spread screening explanation

Benefit requires spatial asymmetry in per-plant environmental dose coefficients and a feasible reallocation range. A linear-regime bound, computed ex ante from the transport kernel by reallocating production cleanest-first within plant limits, predicts both outcomes: a large bound where a large benefit was measured (synthetic, 3.3× dose-coefficient spread), and a small bound where ≈0 was measured (hybrid geometry, 1.5× spread with 40%-of-max turndown floors).

The ex-ante bound values (16.3% synthetic vs 4.3% hybrid) and the dose-spread figures above are documented in docs/CLAIMS.md C5 (diagnosis dated 2026-08-26, post-freeze; reproducible from build_kernel + receptor areas). They are not part of the frozen 2026-08-25 JSON bundle and are cited here for the screening explanation only.
